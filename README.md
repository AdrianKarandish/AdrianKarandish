### A little about me

I'm **Adrian**, co-founder and CEO of **[Adrima](https://adrima.ai)**. I work at the intersection of financial services, applied AI, and product development, building tools that help people make sense of complex information.

Before Adrima, I spent over three years at **RBC Investor Services**, most recently as a **Senior Analyst in US Tax Operations**. That experience shapes how I build: clear processes, reliable data, and software grounded in the way people actually work.

I'm studying **mathematical applications in economics and finance at the University of Toronto**, and I'm based in **Toronto, Canada**.

### What I'm building

**[Adrima](https://adrima.ai) · AI client intelligence**

A platform for financial and document-heavy businesses. We're connecting scattered client information so teams can find answers across documents and systems, with clear references to the sources behind them.

My current focus includes document intelligence, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), OCR, and practical client-service workflows.

**[RiskPulse](https://github.com/AdrianKarandish/RiskPulse) · Financial risk analytics**

A Python CLI and Streamlit app for comparing a stock's risk and performance against the S&P 500. It brings together drawdowns, volatility, downside risk, interactive charts, and downloadable analysis.

`Python` `pandas` `NumPy` `Streamlit` `Plotly`
